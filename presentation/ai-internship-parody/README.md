# The AI Internship (parody video)

A 61-second parody of the *The Internship* video-interview scene, made for the Red Hat x NVIDIA Small Models Hack.

- `ai-internship-parody-v10.mp4`: 1280x720, about 12 MB.
- `SCRIPT.md`: the final script.

How it was made: a still two-shot of the team, animated per scene with Kling 3.0 (via Kie.ai), then dialogue replaced with ElevenLabs voices (John via speech-to-speech in his own voice; the judge and Mark's longer lines via text-to-speech with a laptop-speaker effect on the judge), stitched with ffmpeg. The video, voices and likenesses are AI-generated or AI-altered parody. Mark's voice is a stock voice, not a recording of him.
