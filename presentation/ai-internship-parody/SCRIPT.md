# The AI Internship (parody) — final script

Cast: **John** (SF State cap, left) and **Mark** (white shirt, right). The judges are never seen; only heard as a muffled, laggy voice from the laptop speaker. Taps on the lens are silent actions, never spoken.

Title card: THE AI INTERNSHIP

## 1. Open
- JOHN (into the lens): Is this thing on? *(taps the lens twice)*
- JOHN: Hello, Jensen! Hello, Matt!
- MARK: I'm Mark.
- JOHN: And I'm John.
- JUDGE (unseen): You're muted.
- BOTH: What!

## 2. The pitch
- JOHN: We'd like an internship.
- MARK: Linux since 1997. Worked with Linus Torvalds at the Linux Foundation. Ready to further my career.

## 3. Muted by mistake
- JUDGE (unseen): Tell us about the model you trained.
- JOHN (whispering): Did we train a model?
- MARK (whispering): We're now training AI.
- JUDGE (unseen): We can hear you.
- BOTH: What!

## 4. GPUs
- JUDGE (unseen): Red Hat and NVIDIA ask, how many GPUs?
- JOHN: We were doing DevOps before it was a word.
- MARK (whispering): Do they have GPUs? *(John taps the lens)*

## 5. Project pitch
- JOHN: Can two old guys code like we did at twenty-five, with a fine-tuned Nemotron model?
- MARK: And can NeMo Gym, vLLM and Langfuse help us troubleshoot and ship high-quality software?

End card: NOW WE ARE TRAINING AI. Red Hat x NVIDIA | Small Models Hack
