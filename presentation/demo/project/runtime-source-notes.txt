# Startup experience and dataset lessons

Mark directly reported on October 17, 2026 that many providers were overloaded, the team got off to a slow start, and running the datasets taught them a lot. The first-person startup narration reflects his account. Local execution documents independently record Building/Waiting status, failed initial instance creations, and an unavailable container image. These do not independently establish provider overload as their root cause.

The 7B data report records 39 prefilter rejections and 26 further rows dropped because tokenization/truncation left no assistant labels, producing 188 train plus 30 development rows from 283 source records. This supports the lesson that syntactically valid data can still be unusable for the model. Passing connection or required-tool checks also did not establish successful repository work.
