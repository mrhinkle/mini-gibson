# Limitations

- The v1 comparison used 48 development issues. Its direction favored the adapter, but it did not meet the frozen statistical boundary.
- The tuned model affected substantially more tasks with malformed responses, which blocks promotion.
- The successor R3 experiment was a compatibility preflight. It ran no model, agent, parser, or tests and cannot support a model-quality claim.
- 50 of 456 frozen task packages were incompatible with the current evaluator boundary. The protocol forbids removing or selectively replacing them.
- No base/v1/v2 comparison occurred, so the preregistered formal looks at N=144, N=288, and N=432 were not reached.
- The public 35-task final holdout remains operationally sealed and unconsumed by the pipeline.
- Hosted Langfuse and Jev analysis was not used to score or alter any run. The repository documents a future post-run, redacted integration.
- Cost covers the R3 controller window only. The still-running Brev host continues to incur charges after the evidence cutoff.
