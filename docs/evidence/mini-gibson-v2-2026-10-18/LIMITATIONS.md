# Limitations and exact claim boundary

- The frozen v2 cohort stopped at its first qualification receipt. Only 1 of 456 candidates was executed; it was unqualified, and the remaining 455 were not run.
- Exit code 84 is emitted at the clean-worktree precondition, before product or test patches and before the test command. It proves that `git status` failed or reported tracked changes, but the receipt intentionally preserves only bounded stderr hashes rather than raw stderr, so the narrower subcause cannot be established without a prohibited rerun.
- No v2 adapter was trained. No pilot turn budget was selected. No base/v1/v2 comparative evaluation occurred.
- Formal development sample size is `N=0`. None of the preregistered looks at `N=144`, `N=288`, or `N=432` was reached.
- The 35-task Gibson Bench v0.1 final holdout remained sealed and unconsumed.
- The terminal receipt is evidence that this exact frozen cohort was not executable under the frozen qualification system. It is not evidence for or against the Nemotron Lightning fine-tuning hypothesis.
- The completed v1 result remains the only model comparison: a directional issue-resolution gain that missed its statistical boundary and introduced a significant malformed-response regression. It remains unsuitable for promotion.
- A successor experiment must repair and validate the clean-worktree ownership/state boundary, preflight every frozen image under the same unprivileged runtime, and freeze a new protocol before collecting any new qualification or model outcomes. The terminal task must not be selectively rerun inside this experiment.
